Description draft, to be checked against the final accepted native build:

Build your own little adventure. Pick a vehicle, change its tires and springs, and choose a paint color. Parts change the way your ride climbs, bounces and picks up speed, so you can try a new build on a favorite course.

Explore twelve authored courses with six different vehicles as you earn local course medals. Take your time, retry freely, or try an optional time challenge. In the private track editor, arrange hills, bumps, valleys and bridges, undo a change, and preview the result. Your saved track stays on your device.

The playable base game works offline. It has no ads, purchases, child accounts, chat or public track uploads. Sound and motion options are available in the parent area. Builds, custom tracks and progress are saved locally; there is no cross-device cloud save. This is imaginary arcade play, not real-world driving or mechanical instruction.

Capture 6–8 actual native screenshots per supported device family and localization after layouts and content pass review. |---|---|---|

Record for each file the build commit, platform, device/emulator model, OS, orientation, resolution and exact scene. Label emulator and desktop captures accurately. Do not composite nonexistent UI, use concept art as gameplay, substitute browser screenshots, or crop away a defect to imply a supported layout works. Export PNG/JPEG without alpha where required; preserve untouched source captures for review.

Apple's retrieved screenshot specification permits 1–10 images per device size in JPEG/JPG/PNG and requires applicable iPhone and iPad display sets. The page currently calls for an iPhone with Dynamic Island medium-display set and an iPad 13-inch set when iPad is supported. It lists multiple accepted sizes and scaling rules; select the exact target device row when capturing instead of relying on an older universal size. For example, landscape iPad 13-inch sizes listed include 2752×2064 and 2732×2048. Screenshots must show the app in use under Guideline 2.3.3.

Google's retrieved requirements specify a 512×512 app icon, a 1024×500 feature graphic (JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha), and at least two screenshots for listing publication. Up to eight screenshots per supported device type are allowed. General screenshot bounds are 320–3840 pixels with the longest dimension no more than twice the shortest; the page separately recommends landscape game screenshots at 1920×1080 or larger (three for recommendation eligibility), and device-specific tablet guidance. The requested production set is 6–8; meeting the store minimum does not meet that production requirement.

Final icon source and PNG exports, feature artwork and device capture sets remain gated by actual artifact evidence and rights review. Existing prototype art is a starting reference, not an approved final asset. Check [art/ASSET_LEDGER.md](../art/ASSET_LEDGER.md) before making rights claims.
